How conditions work — quick guide
Each row reads: LEFT value [operator] × multiplier RIGHT value.
· len = how many candles the indicator looks back (SMA len 20 = average of 20 candles).
· ago = candle offset: 0 = current candle, 1 = previous, 3 on 5m candles = 15 minutes ago.
· × = multiplier on the right side: 1.2 = "20% more than", 2 = "double", 0.8 = "20% less".
· % dist from VWAP is signed percent: +0.5 = price 0.5% above VWAP, −0.5 = below. "Near VWAP" = between −0.3 and +0.3.
· crosses ↑/↓ = was on the other side last candle, on this side now.
Example — "volume 20% above its 20-candle average": Price/Volume · volume > × 1.2 SMA · volume · len 20 · ago 1.
