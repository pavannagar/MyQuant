Long-only simulation, per signal: Entry = signal candle's close · Stop-loss = signal candle's low. A trade is LOSS (RR = −1R) the moment a candle's low touches the stop. Without a Take profit (R) set, a trade that never gets stopped stays OPEN — its RR is the live unrealized R multiple from entry to the current price. Set a Take profit (e.g. 4 for 1:4) and a trade instead becomes WIN (RR = the target) once a candle's high reaches it. Max RR is separate from RR — it's the best R multiple ever reached before the stop, target, or "now", so you can see "stopped for −1R, but it touched +2.3R first" or "still open at +0.4R, but peaked at +1.8R". Check 1R → SL to entry to move the stop to breakeven once a trade reaches 1:1 — a later stop-out there is a BREAKEVEN scratch (RR = 0), not counted as a loss. A signal with very few candles elapsed since it fired hasn't had time to play out — read cautiously.
